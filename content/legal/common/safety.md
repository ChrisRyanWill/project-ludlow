# Safety tips

Organizing is legal in most places, but employers do not always behave. A few habits protect you and your coworkers.

- **Use your own phone, on your own mobile data.** Not a work phone, work computer or work Wi-Fi. Employers can often see what happens on those.
- **Do not use work email, Slack or chat** for anything about the union.
- **Do not sign or organize while on the clock.**
- **Talk in person** for anything sensitive. Do not put names in group chats.
- **Lock your phone** and keep your member link somewhere private (a password manager, or a Signal "Note to Self").
- **Use "Quick exit"** (the button at the top of every page, or press Esc three times) if someone walks up.
- **Keep a private record** of anything that looks like punishment for organizing. Only you can read it unless you choose to share it.
- **Trust your coworkers, but check.** New people join by a link from someone you know, and a group link only counts once the person who shared it confirms them in person.
