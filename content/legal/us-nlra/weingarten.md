# Weingarten rights: ask for your union representative

If your employer calls you into a meeting and you reasonably believe the meeting could lead to discipline, you have the right to ask for a union representative to be there. This comes from a U.S. Supreme Court case, *NLRB v. J. Weingarten, Inc.* (1975).

**What to say:** "If this discussion could lead to my being disciplined, I request that a union representative be present. Until my representative is here, I choose not to answer questions."

- The meeting is paused until your representative arrives, or you may choose to end it.
- You must ask. The employer does not have to remind you.
- Stay calm and polite. Do not lie. You may still be required to do your job.
