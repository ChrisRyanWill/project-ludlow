# Your rights, in plain words

Across the world, workers have the right to form and join unions and to bargain collectively. This is set out in the International Labour Organization's Conventions 87 (Freedom of Association) and 98 (Right to Organise and Collective Bargaining), which most countries have accepted. What that means in practice, who is covered, and what happens if an employer breaks the rules all depend on the law where you live.
