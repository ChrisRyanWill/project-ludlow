# Your rights, in plain words (United Kingdom)

In the UK, workers have the right to join a trade union and to take part in its activities, and it is unlawful for an employer to dismiss or treat you badly for that reason. Workers also have the right to be accompanied by a trade union representative or a colleague at disciplinary and grievance hearings.

## If something happens
1. Stay calm.
2. Write down what happened, when, and who saw it.
3. Tell your union or organizing committee, and ask for advice from a union, Acas or a solicitor. Time limits are short.

Learn more at [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk) and [acas.org.uk](https://www.acas.org.uk).
