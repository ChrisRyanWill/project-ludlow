# Next steps (United Kingdom)

1. **Get advice.** Contact a recognised trade union, Acas or an employment solicitor before you send anything.
2. **Ask the employer first.** The statutory process starts with a written request to the employer for recognition. TODO(lawyer): confirm the exact form of the request.
3. **If the employer refuses or does not respond**, the union can apply to the CAC. From 6 April 2026 the union no longer has to show that a majority of the group is *likely* to support recognition. The union must still show a minimum level of membership in the group (10% at the time of writing).
4. **Ballot or automatic recognition.** If more than half the group are already members, the CAC can generally recognise the union without a ballot. Otherwise there is a ballot. From 6 April 2026 the union wins a ballot with a simple majority of votes cast; the old rule that at least 40% of the whole group must vote yes has gone.
5. **Expect it to take months.** The process was described as taking roughly 22 weeks.
