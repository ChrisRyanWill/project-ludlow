# Code of Conduct

Project Ludlow is a tool for people who are organizing together, sometimes at real personal risk. We hold ourselves to the standards of a good union meeting: everyone gets heard, disagreement is welcome, and nobody gets hurt for speaking up.

## What we expect

- **Be respectful and assume good faith.** Argue with ideas, not people. Strategy and technical disagreements are healthy; contempt is not.
- **Include people.** Welcome newcomers, explain jargon, and remember that many contributors are organizers, translators and lawyers, not programmers.
- **Give credit and take feedback gracefully.** Reviews are about the work.
- **Protect people's safety.**
  - Never post real member lists, real workplaces, real names, or screenshots of real campaigns, including to illustrate a bug. Use invented data.
  - Never ask anyone to test with real people's data.
  - Do not try to identify or expose anyone who has signed a card or joined a campaign.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sustained disruption, sexual attention, and spam. Using this project to attack, surveil or retaliate against workers or organizers is grounds for an immediate, permanent ban.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainers by opening a **private security advisory** on the repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability") and titling it "Conduct report". Only maintainers can see it. We will listen, look into it fairly, and keep your report confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and warn, suspend or ban people to keep the project safe. If a maintainer is the subject of a report, the remaining maintainers (or the repository owner) will handle it.

*Written for this project. We welcome improvements.*
